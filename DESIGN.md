# Chotu Restro AI — Warm Espresso & Terracotta Style Guide

> An inviting, tactile, modern hospitality palette balancing linen-like warm neutrals with appetite-stimulating burnt terracotta accents.

## 60-30-10 Hospitality Color Ratio

| Element | Color Name | Hex Code | Ratio | Role in Experience |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Background Canvas** | Warm Alabaster | `#FAF8F5` | 60% | Organic, calm foundation mimicking unbleached linen and warm paper |
| **Cards & Surfaces** | Soft Oat | `#EFECE6` | 30% | Tactile component tiles, table ledgers, KDS cards, and input fields |
| **Primary Typography** | Deep Espresso | `#1E1B18` | - | High-contrast legibility across bright counters and ambient dining rooms |
| **Muted & Secondary** | Warm Taupe | `#6E675F` | - | Timestamps, station tags, helper notes, and table capacities |
| **Pop Accent & CTAs** | Burnt Terracotta | `#D95D39` | 10% | Add to Cart, Fire KOT, Active Dispatch pills, and interactive highlights |
| **Linework & Borders** | Oat Stone | `#DDD6CC` | - | Architectural structural dividers |
